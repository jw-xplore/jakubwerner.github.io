PAKLYX is a turn-based puzzle game about finding the correct sequence of moves. Use special buttons to manipulate timers, delay them, and collect all keys in the level. Play as Paklyx, a curious keyhole in a world where button mechanisms have replaced keys. Plan the right move sequence, trigger buttons, and collect keys on the exact turn they appear. Progress through levels and uncover special button types that introduce new rules to master.

I have joined the Project later in the development to help prepare game for the Steam Next Fest demo. My main contribution was a settings menu and localization implementation. Later on I added several puzzle mechanics, namely portals, repeat tiles and ghost movement. I have worked closely with the game designer Patrik Chleboun to implement and iterate features based on planned design.
